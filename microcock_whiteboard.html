Tools: V select, P pen, H highlighter, L temporary laser, E eraser, T text, S shape
Colors: 1-8   Size: [ and ]
Zoom: mouse wheel, Ctrl+wheel, + / -   Reset: 0
Pan: right-drag (any tool), M (hand tool, left-drag), Space+drag, Shift+wheel (horizontal), Alt+wheel (vertical), arrow keys. All work mid-stroke.
Select: click, Shift+click, drag a box, Ctrl+A. Double-click a text box to edit.
Text: Ctrl+B bold, Ctrl+U underline (inside a box), Esc finishes editing
Layers: Ctrl+] forward, Ctrl+[ back, add Shift for front/back
Other: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo, Ctrl+D duplicate, Delete, G grid, paste or drop images, Shift constrains shapes
